Studio notes

One studio. Five things built.

I built these products one real problem at a time: reading a chart, finding a search obstacle, comparing model responses, tracing a tool call, or matching a refund to a payout. Each has a focused pilot so you can bring a concrete example and see what it does.

The type came from VedaaChari.

I brought its Fraunces headlines and Hind body type across the studio. Fraunces gives each product a distinct headline. Hind keeps longer explanations and Devanagari easy to read.

The artwork has a job.

I used actual product screens where available and labeled sample diagrams for SEO and REA. On scroll, the artwork moves gently into place while the text stays put.
